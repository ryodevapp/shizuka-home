Shizuka: Quiet Home Screen — Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy (the "Policy") sets out how information is handled in the iOS application "Shizuka: Quiet Home Screen" (the "App").

1. Basic policy

The App does not send the apps you place on your Home Screen, your Screen Time information, or the usage records shown in the look-back off your device. These are used only on your device.

We run no server, and you do not create an account in the App. We do not collect any information about you. We never obtain your name, email address, phone number, address, location, any identifier of your device, or any usage data.

The only communication the App makes is with Apple's App Store, for purchasing the paid version and restoring purchases (section 5).

When you add Breath Before Opening (a 10-second deep breath before an app opens) for an app, you open a shared link to a shortcut that we prepared, which is hosted on Apple's iCloud, in the iPhone's Shortcuts app. The Shortcuts app fetches the contents of the link. The App does not send any information about you at that time.

2. Information stored on your device

The App stores your settings and usage records only on that device. What it stores is as follows.

  • The apps on your Home Screen (the name shown on the Home Screen and the app it opens)
  • Settings for switching by time of day made in an earlier version (names of time periods, start times, and the apps to list), and the date and time you first became able to use switching by time of day
  • Focus time settings and records (the apps that stay closed during Focus time, and the time you focused). The "apps to list on the Home Screen during focus" chosen in an earlier version only remain on the device and are not used in the current version.
  • Settings for daily Focus time periods made in an earlier version (the time periods and the apps that stay closed during them). They are not used in the current version and only remain on the device.
  • Records of "Open for 5 minutes" from an earlier version (an iOS-assigned marker pointing to the app that was opened, which daily Focus time period it was opened in, the time it was opened, and the time it would be blocked again). The current version does not create new ones.
  • Breath Before Opening settings and records (the target apps; the time the breath screen appeared and the app; the time you stopped yourself from opening on the breath screen and the running total of times you stopped; the time of the last opening after a breath; whether the first-time note has been shown; the apps that skip the breath screen (apps turned off); the on/off state of "Use Breath Before Opening"; and the waiting time chosen in an earlier version)
  • Appearance settings (theme, and whether to show the date and time line)
  • Progress through setup (whether setup is finished and when), and the done marks for "Make it quieter" (which of wallpaper, other pages, the Breath Before Opening setting, and notifications you have finished)
  • Plan status (free, in Trial, or paid version), the date and time the Trial ends, whether you have received the Trial, and the date and time the purchase record was last checked
  • The date and time the "Trial has ended" notice was shown, and the date and time the App Store review request was shown

The settings for switching by time of day and the waiting time chosen in an earlier version were made in an earlier version and remain on the device. The current version does not use them. Whether to show the date and time line is used for the widget's display.

The time of the last opening after a breath is used to avoid showing the breath screen again for the same app for 5 minutes after you open it, and to show the time "since you last opened it" on the breath screen. The time the breath screen appeared and the app are used to show how many times you tried to open that app today; entries older than 8 days are deleted the next time something is recorded. The running total of times you stopped is used to decide when to show the App Store review request. Whether the first-time note has been shown is used to add a short note only the first time the breath screen appears. The apps that skip the breath screen are used so the breath screen does not appear when you open those apps. While "Use Breath Before Opening" is off, the breath screen does not appear for any app. When you view the breath screen with "Try it", no time or count is recorded.

Records of "Open for 5 minutes" from an earlier version stay only on your device and are not sent anywhere. The App deletes a record one day after the time it was due to block again, when a later Focus session starts or ends, for example.

The date and time the Trial ends is used to return you to the free range at that time. Whether you have received the Trial decides whether the purchase screen offers the Trial. The date and time the purchase record was last checked is used to decide how long the paid version keeps working when the App Store's purchase record cannot be read for a while.

The date and time the "Trial has ended" notice was shown and the date and time the review request was shown are each used to show them only once. To keep the two from appearing around the same time, the review request is not shown while the notice is expected to appear, nor for 24 hours after the notice is shown. These dates and times are also stored only on your device and are not sent anywhere.

This content is kept in storage on the device (an App Group) that the App itself, the widget, and the Screen Time extensions share. It stays on your device and is not sent to us or to any third party. We cannot see it.

This content is deleted together with the App when you delete the App from your device.

3. Screen Time information

The "apps that stay closed" used by Focus time are chosen by you in a selection screen that Apple provides. The App receives only an iOS-assigned marker that points to each chosen app. The app names and icons shown on the screen are used for display only, inside a frame iOS provides, and are neither stored nor sent. The marker is stored on your device and is never sent off the device.

The time you spent on your phone and the usage time of the apps you used most, shown in the look-back, are drawn inside a frame iOS provides. The usage data does not reach the App itself and never leaves your device. We cannot see this data.

The "times you stopped yourself from opening" and "time you focused" shown in the look-back are recorded by the App on your device (section 2).

4. iCloud Backup

If you have iCloud Backup turned on for your device, the data stored on your device as listed in section 2 may be copied to Apple's iCloud as part of the device backup.

This copy is not made by the App. iOS makes it as part of its backup feature. The handling of backed-up content follows Apple's terms. You can change whether backups are made at any time in the iOS Settings app.

5. Information about purchases

The purchase of the paid version and the 14-day Trial are handled through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment information, and it is not passed to us. We do not receive or keep payment details such as your credit card number.

Whether the paid version is active, and whether you are within the Trial period, is decided on your device: the App reads the App Store's purchase record through iOS. The App does not send the purchase record to us or to any third party.

What the App stores on your device is the state (free, in Trial, or paid version), the date and time the Trial ends, whether you have received the Trial, the date and time the purchase record was last checked, and the date and time the "Trial has ended" notice was shown (section 2). These are also kept only on your device and are not sent anywhere.

6. Sharing with third parties, and advertising

Because we do not collect information about you, we do not share it with third parties either.

We may look at aggregate statistics that Apple provides, which do not identify individuals (such as the number of downloads). Apple produces these statistics. The App does not send information to us.

The App shows no advertising. The App includes no external libraries, including any for advertising or measurement.

The App does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not track you across other apps or websites. For that reason it does not show the iOS "tracking permission" prompt.

7. Permissions for device features

The App asks for your permission through iOS when it uses the following features.

  • Screen Time: asked the first time you use Focus time, or when you tap "Allow" in the look-back. You can withdraw the permission in the iOS Settings app. If you withdraw it, Focus time does not work and the time you spent on your phone no longer appears in the look-back.
  • Notifications: used to tell you that a focus session has ended. Notifications are created on your device and are not sent from outside.
  • Photos: the App asks for add-only access, to save a wallpaper in the same color as your Home Screen to Photos. The App never reads your photos.
